Criteria & Governance

Institutional Eligibility & Requirements

We work exclusively with registered, accredited educational institutions with verifiable operational history.

Institutional Qualification Criteria

✓

Registered Corporate Entity

Private school registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) under Part A (Company Limited by Shares) or Part B (Business Name).

✓

Ministry Approval / Accreditation

Valid registration, approval, or WAEC/NECO center code from the State Ministry of Education.

✓

Minimum 2 Years Operational Track Record

Institution has successfully completed at least two consecutive academic sessions with verifiable student enrollment.

✓

Active Student Population

Demonstrable student population ensuring viable tuition fee collection coverage over the requested borrowing tenure.

Documentation Checklist

1

CAC Certificate & Status Report

Certificate of registration and Status Report showing trustees, directors, or proprietor.

2

12 Months Primary School Bank Statements

Official bank statements demonstrating tuition deposit inflow patterns from parents and guardians.

3

Student Enrollment & Fee Schedule

Breakdown of student counts by class and official published termly tuition tariffs.

4

Board Resolution & Valid Identification

Board / Proprietor resolution authorizing the credit request and valid government ID (NIN / International Passport) of signatories.
